# Change table: Ordo Missae 1962 → 1970 (Low Mass)

Fact base for `src/content/missal.js`. **Needs user approval before Task 4.**

- **Vetus:** *Missale Romanum* 1962, Ordo Missae and *Ritus servandus*, Low Mass. 1960 Code of Rubrics applies.
- **Novus:** *Missale Romanum* 1970, Ordo Missae, with the *Institutio Generalis* (GIRM) 1969/1970.
- **Stages:** IO = *Inter Oecumenici* (26 Sep 1964, in force 7 Mar 1965); TAA = *Tres abhinc annos* (4 May 1967, in force 29 Jun 1967); OM69 = *Ordo Missae* promulgated 1969, in force with MR1970.
- References are given at the level of the named rite or prayer (e.g. "OM1970, Ritus initiales"). Paragraph numbers for the GIRM are added only after being checked against the Latin text. None are guessed here.
- Confidence: **high** = checked against the order of Mass itself or a primary document; **medium** = from the rubrics as commonly printed, still to be checked line by line against the editio typica; **low** = excluded from the site unless resolved.
- Incense rites are omitted throughout (Low Mass).

## Deliberately NOT shown as 1970 changes

| Item | Why |
|---|---|
| Leonine prayers after Low Mass | Not part of the 1962 Ordo Missae; suppressed by IO 48j (1964). Out of scope. |
| Second Confiteor before the people's Communion | Already dropped by the 1960 Code of Rubrics, so absent from the 1962 missal. |
| Communion kneeling / on the tongue | Not settled by the 1970 Ordo itself (later indults and local norms). Out of scope. |
| Priest facing the people | Neither missal mandates it; the 1970 Ordo gives rubrics "turned to the people" at points but does not require that orientation. Excluded rather than overstated. |
